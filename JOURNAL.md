# October 18, 2026: 18/10/2026 First Journal/The Start
<!-- fabricate:entry 665 -->

**This is my first microcontroller**, it's based of an stm32. First I did lots of research and started the project with schematics. I didn't finish the schematics, but hopefully I will tomorrow or soon. I am using documentation from [st.com](https://www.st.com/content/st_com/en.html) It took me a while to find and read the needed parts, but I found it. All went well, just one part was not included in the default KiCad library so I found it on [ultralibrarian.com](https://www.ultralibrarian.com/)
**Why did I choose to make a microcontroller?**
I choose to make a microcontroller, because I think it's a good opener to bigger and more complex projects. In the past I have made a few projects, like a [3x3 Keypad (MinBoard)](https://github.com/LaurynasLubauskas/MinBoard) and I enjoyed making those projects.
**What is the end goal?**
From Fabricate I want to earn a 3d printer, so this wont be my only project that I will make here. I have a few ideas for other projects, but that's for future journals.
